Problem statement02 / 21

Responders need to know where buildings collapsed, and how many, within hours and on the hardware they actually have.

Count, don't guess

A VLM describes a scene well but cannot count. In 499A, VLM-only building counting fell 10.62 points below the untuned base model.

Find the rare class

Destroyed buildings are 0.33% of pixels. Our 499A U-Net reached 0.17 IoU on them, and about 4 of 5 pixels it called destroyed were false alarms.

Run where responders are

No paid API, no internet, small GPUs. The 499A report generator calls the Gemini API, and our merged 7B model is about 15 GB in FP16.

499B goala hybrid system that counts from pixels, reasons in language, and runs offline on free or small GPUs.

Review · Gaps06 / 21

Four gaps carried into 499B

01
Spatial blindness

The VLM sees at most 512 vision tokens per image on a T4, so small structures vanish. Building counting fell to 23.58%, below the base model's 34.2%.

02
A weak spatial stage

Destroyed buildings are 0.33% of pixels and absent from 63% of images. The U-Net's Destroyed IoU was 0.166, at 0.18 precision.

03
Cloud dependency

DisasTeller's vision agent calls the Gemini API, so the report generator stops where the network is down.

04
Too big for the field

The merged 7B model is about 15 GB in FP16. Field laptops need a model that fits in roughly 6 GB.

Why the segmentation stage comes first: every hybrid count, collage and heatmap inherits the U-Net's errors.

The 15-day segmentation ablation sprint

Starting point: U-Net with ResNet-34, CE + Dice loss, images resized to 512, at mIoU 0.4619. Each track changed exactly one variable; the lead then audited every result.

Track A · Aryan Sami
Data imbalance

Weighted sampler (10× / 5× / 1× by rarest class) and copy-paste of damaged and destroyed cut-outs, in 10-epoch screens.

Track B · Tamanna Akter Mou
Architecture

U-Net++, U-Net++ with scSE attention, DeepLabV3+ and MA-Net against the U-Net, all with CE + Dice.

Track C · Abdullah Al Noman
Loss and backbone

Focal Tversky (two settings), phased Focal Tversky → Lovász-Softmax, then ResNet-50 and EfficientNet-B4 encoders.

Track D · Ridita Afrin Riya
Resolution and inference

Native 512 and 768 random crops instead of resizing, 8-view test-time augmentation and a sliding window.

LeadAbrar Mohammed Tanzim Alam: sprint plan, results audit and the shared champion protocol (D4).

499B so far · Audit12 / 21

Making the sprint's numbers comparable

What the audit found
  • Three evaluation setups: resized 512 images (A–C), random crops (D), 10-epoch screens (A)
  • One copy-paste run drew cut-outs from validation images
  • Architecture runs also changed batch size and learning rate
  • Checkpoints were picked on the same validation set used for reporting
  • One seed per run, so a +0.002 gain cannot be told from noise
  • Summary table rebuilt from the notebooks' saved outputs
The E-protocol, used for every champion run
  • The same 645 validation images, scored at full native resolution, deterministically
  • One dataset-level confusion matrix: per-class IoU, precision, recall, F1 and F2
  • The last epoch is reported; validation never picks the checkpoint
  • A seed-noise bar from a repeated run (R1 vs R6)
  • Old checkpoints re-scored the same way (E0)

Future work · From the 499A roadmap15 / 21

Grounding and shrinking the VLM

Resolution unlocking
What

Lift the 512-token image cap at inference only, where no gradients are stored.

Why

Deviation D4 drove the counting (−10.62), road-damage (−3.06) and relational (−1.40) regressions.

Measure

Re-score the six tasks on larger free GPUs (Kaggle P100, Colab L4).

Textual bridge prompting
What

Give the VLM the U-Net's findings as text: counts per damage class, damaged share and quadrant locations.

Why

The VLM reasons over exact numbers instead of resolving tiny structures itself.

Measure

Building-counting accuracy against the base model's 34.2%.

7B → 3B distillation
What

The fine-tuned 7B teacher writes step-by-step rationales; a Qwen2.5-VL-3B student learns from them with our QLoRA pipeline.

Why

A model that fits rescue laptops, about 6 GB.

Status

Teacher and student notebooks written; runs pending.

We asked another LLM to answer open‑ended questions, where the model was instructed to provide its answers in a json format as the following: {{"Model_answer": <model_output>}}  
Your job as a judge is to check whether the model’s exact answer phrase has the same meaning as any of the ground‑truth answers.

Input:
  Question: {question}
  Model Output: {model_output}
  Ground‑Truth Answers: {ground_truth}

Instructions:
  - Read the question, model output, and the groundtruth answers.
  - Extract the exact answer phrase from the model’s raw output.
  - Map the extracted phrase to its core meaning, matching:
      • Synonyms  
      • Number words vs. digits  
      • Multi‑word equivalents  
      • Combinations of ground‑truth terms (e.g., “building structure” matches both “building” and “structure”)
  - Compare the mapped meaning against the ground‑truth answers.
  - Decide “Right” if there is a match, otherwise “Wrong”.
  - Output exactly one JSON object with:
    {{"decision": "<Right|Wrong>", "answer": "<the exact answer phrase>"}}
  - Do not include any additional text or explanation.
